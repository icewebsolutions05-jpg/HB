Our Story

The HB began with a simple idea: food should taste like where it comes from. Every pot of jollof, every skewer of suya and every bowl of egusi starts with the spices and slow cooking our families have trusted for generations. We opened our doors in Lagos to serve that taste in the city that inspired it. By day, a table full of proper Nigerian cooking. By night, the music turns up, the lounge fills, and the same plates travel around with a drink in hand. Come hungry. Come with friends. Taste where it all began.
